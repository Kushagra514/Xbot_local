# Softmax turns logits into probabilities

Softmax is the layer that turns a vector of raw logits into a probability distribution that sums to 1.

The math: e^(z_i) / sum(e^(z_j)). Subtracting the max logit before exponentiating keeps it numerically stable (no overflow).

One concrete example: logits [2.0, 1.0, 0.1] → softmax → [0.659, 0.242, 0.099]. The highest logit dominates but the others still get a share.

Why it matters: cross-entropy loss then measures how far this distribution is from the one-hot target — that's the gradient signal that trains the classifier.
