# RLHF in 5 tweets

1/ Problem: base language models autocomplete text, they don't follow instructions. They'll ramble or refuse.

2/ Idea: learn a reward model from human preferences, then optimize the policy against it.

3/ Method: humans rank model outputs → reward model predicts the score → PPO fine-tunes the policy to maximize reward.

4/ Result: models that follow instructions and refuse harmful requests.

5/ My take: RLHF aligns style, not truth. A rewarded answer can still be confidently wrong.
