# LoRA fine-tuning: the rank bottleneck

Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) freezes the base model and trains only small low-rank matrices A and B instead of the full weight matrix.

The bug I hit: I set the LoRA rank r=1 for a 7B model and the loss stopped decreasing. The update space was too small to capture the task.

The fix: bump r to 8 or 16. Rank controls how much task-specific information the adapter can store — too low and it underfits, too high and you lose the parameter-efficiency benefit.

Rule of thumb: start at r=8, alpha=16, and monitor validation loss.
